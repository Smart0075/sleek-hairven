About

Styled by Esty is a beauty brand focused on professional wig services, including wig making, wig installation, revamping, and styling. The brand is known for helping clients achieve elegant and confident looks through quality hair services. James Esther. popularly known as ESTY. graduated from Ambrose Alli University (AAU) in the Department of Public Administration. She is from Igbanke in Edo State, Nigeria. With passion and creativity, she continues to grow Styled by Esty into a trusted name in the hair and beauty industry.
